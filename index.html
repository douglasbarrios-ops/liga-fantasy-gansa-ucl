Scoring. League phase: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw. Bonuses: +3 for finishing in the top 8, +1 for finishing 9th to 24th, +1 for surviving the 9–24 playoff and reaching the round of 16. The two playoff legs award no match points and no goals. From the round of 16 onward every leg is worth 3 / 1 / 0, with no further bonuses for advancing. Penalty shootouts award nothing: a leg settled from the spot is scored as a draw. Extra time counts toward the result.

Ties. Managers level on points are separated by goal difference, then goals scored, then alphabetically. Goals are totalled across all four clubs in every match that awards points — the league phase and the round of 16 onward. Playoff legs are excluded, as they are for points.
